People assurance

The uniform is visible. The process behind it matters more.

Recruitment, verification and training form a controlled path from requirement to deployment. That trust chain is more meaningful than simply saying “trained guards.”

Recruit to the assignment

Role, client, location, fitness, experience and deployment requirements shape selection.

Verify before confirmation

Document checks, CNIC/NADRA verification, local police and special-branch verification support screening.

Train for security responsibilities

Guard duties, access control, search, emergency response, weapons handling, first aid and conduct.

Prepare, deploy and reinforce

Site briefing and refresher training support consistency as responsibilities change.
